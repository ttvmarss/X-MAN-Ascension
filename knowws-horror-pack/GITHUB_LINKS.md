### Quick install

1. Open the `.mcaddon` link above
2. Minecraft imports both behavior and resource packs automatically
3. Go to **Settings → Storage → your world → Manage**
4. Add **KNOWWS Horror Pack BP** and enable **Beta APIs**, **Holiday Creator Features**, **Custom Biomes**
5. Re-enter your world
